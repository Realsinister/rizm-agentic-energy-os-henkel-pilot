01b. Anti-Hallucination SSoT Layer & Multi-Agent Management

Critical industrial AI safety requirement: Combined Heat & Power assets must NEVER hallucinate physical capacities or thermal turn-down limits.

🛡️
Single Source of Truth (SSoT) Layer
Eliminates AI Hallucinations: Enforces a deterministic SSoT layer (SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md & site_config.json). The AI agent pipeline never generates raw unverified dispatch commands; all optimization decisions are bounded by strict mathematical equations in a PuLP Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP), guaranteeing 0% AI hallucination risk.
🤖
Multi-Agent Environment Orchestration
Modular Agentic Workflow: Orchestrated specialized parallel subagents (Research Agent, Optimization Agent, Telemetry Validator Agent, Presentation UI Agent). Implemented strict contract-based inter-agent communication, scoped file permissions, and automated verification suites (pytest tests/test_suite.py) to eliminate race conditions and state drift.
02. End-to-End Fail-Proof Pipeline Architecture

Interactive step-by-step workflow showing how raw telemetry flows into SSoT validation, PuLP MILP optimization, Monte Carlo sensitivity sweeps, and C-suite presentation layers.

1
Telemetry Synthesis & Ingestion src/data_generator.py
Synthesizes realistic 96-interval (15-minute, 24-hour) EPEX SPOT price curves featuring mid-day solar price crashes (-€15 to +€10/MWh), electrical demand (45 MW), thermal spray-drying demand (60 MWt), and district heating export capacity (15–30 MWt).
2
Telemetry Sanitation & Quality Assurance src/data_validator.py
Fail-proof telemetry validator. Automatically imputes missing timestamps via linear interpolation, clips negative load sensor glitches, caps extreme outliers, and ensures data integrity before optimization.
3
SSoT-Bound Deterministic MILP Solver (0% Hallucination) src/optimizer.py & SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md
Formulates a PuLP MILP model strictly constrained by SSoT physical equations. Optimizes 84 MW CHP dispatch over 96 intervals. Calculates exact daily OpEx savings (€5,981.71/day base), normalized unit savings (€5.46 / metric ton), and daily CO2 reduction (38.12 tCO2e/day).
4
Multi-Variable Monte Carlo Sensitivity Engine src/sensitivity_engine.py
Executes a 100-scenario parameter sweep across natural gas prices (€25–€60/MWh), EU ETS carbon shadow prices (€50–€140/tCO2), and spot volatility (0.8x–1.5x). Establishes P10 (€1.48/t) to P90 (€22.13/t) confidence bounds.
5
Verifiable Audit Ledger & CI/CD Pipeline data/AUDIT_LEDGER.json & .github/workflows/ci.yml
Exports a cryptographically verifiable JSON audit trail ledger. Integrated with GitHub Actions CI/CD to run automated pytest verification suites (`tests/test_suite.py`) on every commit.
6
Executive Presentation Layer & 1-Click Batch Launcher src/app.py & Sample Energy OS.bat
Interactive Streamlit C-suite presentation app featuring a 1-click `Sample Energy OS.bat` launcher, `🔄 Reset to Default Parameters` button, cumulative cost area charts, hourly savings bar charts, and executive briefing tabs.

04. First On-Site Visit Strategy (1-Day Audit Playbook)

How an FDE avoids operational friction by asking for the single most load-bearing data request and meeting the single key stakeholder.

📊
Single Load-Bearing Data Request
15-minute sub-metered high-pressure steam flow logs for spray-drying towers alongside CHP gas consumption and electrical feed data over 12 months.

Why? High-level monthly utility bills lack temporal resolution. Without 15-minute thermal sub-metering, flexibility algorithms cannot calculate thermal turn-down constraints.
🤝
Single Key Stakeholder (30-Min Meeting)
Plant Operations Manager / Head of Site Energy Utilities (Dipl.-Ing. Anke Kappenhagen / Dr. Daniel Kleine).

Why? Corporate energy procurement and plant operations work in isolated silos. The Plant Manager holds sole cross-cutting authority to approve asset flexibility without violating production volume SLAs.
