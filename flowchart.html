🎯 Challenge Question Answered: How do we normalize energy savings to core business SLAs?
Henkel's global flagship headquarters in Düsseldorf-Holthausen produces 400,000 metric tons of detergent and adhesives annually. The baseline daily slurry throughput is 1,095.89 metric tons/day. All energy savings are normalized to € / metric ton of product saved.
Daily Detergent SLA = 400,000 tons / 365 days = 1,095.89 tons/day Normalized Unit Metric (€/ton) = €5,981.71 / 1,095.89 tons = €5.46 / ton
