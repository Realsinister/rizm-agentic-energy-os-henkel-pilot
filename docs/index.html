Henkel Düsseldorf Flagship Plant SLA
Henkel's global headquarters plant in Düsseldorf-Holthausen produces 400,000 metric tons/year of Laundry & Home Care products and industrial adhesives.
Daily Detergent Throughput (Q_daily) = 400,000 tons / 365 days = 1,095.89 metric tons/day Normalized Unit Savings (€/ton) = Daily Savings (€5,981.71) / 1,095.89 tons = €5.46 / ton

First On-Site Meeting Strategy: Schedule 30 minutes with the Plant Operations Manager (Dipl.-Ing. Anke Kappenhagen) to request 15-minute sub-metered steam flow logs for spray-drying towers.

Captive 84 MW Gas CHP Power Station
The captive Combined Heat & Power (CHP) plant has an electrical capacity of 84 MW, minimum stable load of 20 MW, electrical efficiency of 42%, and heat-to-power ratio of 1.20.
Thermal Output Equation: H_chp(t) = 1.20 * P_chp(t) Electrical Balance Constraint: P_chp(t) + P_grid(t) == Electrical_Demand(t) Gas Consumption Cost: Fuel_Cost = (P_chp / 0.42) * C_gas

During mid-day EPEX spot solar crashes (11:30–15:30), Sample Energy OS automatically throttles CHP generation to 20 MW and imports cheap grid power.

Stadtwerke Düsseldorf District Heating Export Link
In April 2026, Henkel, Stadtwerke Düsseldorf, and Bilfinger commissioned a 700 m² waste-heat energy center with a 3.6 km pipeline link exporting excess thermal energy into Garath/Benrath.
Thermal Balance Constraint: H_chp(t) + H_boiler(t) == Thermal_Demand(t) + H_dh(t) District Heat Export Cap: 0 <= H_dh(t) <= District_Heating_Capacity_MWt(t) Feed-in Revenue: Heat_Revenue = H_dh(t) * R_dh (€28/MWh)

Zero-Hallucination SSoT Layer (`SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`)
Combats AI hallucination in mission-critical chemical power plants by binding all dispatch decisions to a deterministic PuLP Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP).
# Deterministic PuLP MILP Objective Function Min Daily_OpEx = Sum_{t=1..96} [ (P_chp[t] / eta_elec) * C_gas + (H_boiler[t] / eta_boiler) * C_boiler + P_grid[t] * Spot_Price[t] - H_dh[t] * R_dh + Carbon_Emissions[t] * C_co2 ] * delta_t
